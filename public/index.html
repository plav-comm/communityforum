Our Mission

Community is what binds us together as a people.

1

To provide Community-SPAN's audience access to every local town and city government proceeding across the country, and to other local forums where local public policy is discussed, debated and decided — all without editing, commentary or analysis, and with a balanced presentation of points of view.

2

To provide elected and appointed officials at every level of government, and others who would influence public policy, a direct conduit to the audience without filtering or otherwise distorting their points of view.

We build interest in local participation, promote civic duty by holding up those already serving as role models, and foster the notion that we are all members of a community — our own local, our state and our country.

Community-Span

A content platform aggregating town, city, county and school district government feeds from around the country — giving anyone the ability to look in on their own community, and everyone else's.
